OrderFlow

An AI-powered order operations assistant for small businesses and e-commerce sellers.

The Kernel: OrderFlow inverts the usual dashboard. Instead of showing everything and letting you figure out what matters, it reads every order's state, determines priority automatically, and surfaces a plain-language explanation with a clear recommended action. The seller doesn't scan — they respond.

Who It's For

🧑‍💼

Small Business Owner / Solo E-Commerce Seller

Receives orders from both an online store and WhatsApp. Today they switch between a website backend, a WhatsApp thread, and maybe a spreadsheet — spending more time figuring out what to do next than actually doing it. OrderFlow gives them one place to see everything and tells them what to handle first.

Core Loop

1

Seller opens OrderFlow

Dashboard: total orders, attention-needed count, critical/high count, top recommended actions.

2

Priority is surfaced automatically

Every order is scored and labelled. The most urgent ones surface at the top with a short explanation.

"High priority — customer has been waiting 2 hours for confirmation."
3

Seller opens the most urgent order

Full detail view: what, where, status, why, next action, and one clear action button.

4

Seller takes the guided action

Accept · Mark Processing · Mark Ready · Mark Shipped · Complete · Contact Customer.

5

Dashboard updates, next order surfaces

Status changes immediately. The next recommended action appears. Repeat until the queue is clear.
